# PM SHRI KV Sulur DLMS Community Moderation Policy

**Effective: 18 October 2026**

Our community is a place for students, teachers, and library staff to discuss books, share original writing, ask questions, and help one another. Moderation should protect that space while allowing normal conversation.

## 1. Keep discussion respectful and school-appropriate

- Treat other members kindly. Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, slurs, sexual content, and sharing another person's private information are not allowed.
- Keep posts relevant to learning, reading, school activities, and the library community.
- Report a concern instead of retaliating. Staff review reports and may remove content or contact the member involved.

## 2. Do not flood the community

- Ordinary greetings, short answers, and friendly conversation are welcome. A single “hi” or “thanks” is not, by itself, spam.
- Do not flood a post or discussion with repeated words or phrases (for example, “hi hi hi”), repeatedly submit the same message, or post content only to disrupt the feed.
- A short posting cooldown is used to manage bursts. Reaching that pace limit alone is not misconduct and does not add a warning.
- For clear repeated-content spam, the system gives **three ordinary reminders** to stop and edit the message. These reminders are tracked with the account, not just the device.

## 3. Write original work and credit sources

- Posts, answers, stories, poems, and other creative work must be your own or clearly credited.
- Do not copy another member's post or someone else's work and present it as your own.
- When using a short quotation, use quotation marks and name or link the author/source. Explain the idea in your own words where possible.
- A post that closely matches recent community content may be stopped before it is published. Rewrite it or add clear attribution. This automatic check only compares content already loaded from the community feed; it cannot reliably detect every copied work on the wider internet. Members and staff can report suspected plagiarism for review.

## 4. Escalating actions

For repeated clear spam, or continued/serious violations after review:

1. **Three ordinary reminders** are given for clear repetitive spam. Posting speed by itself does not count toward these reminders.
2. **Formal warning 1:** community posting is paused for 24 hours.
3. **Formal warning 2:** community posting is paused for 48 hours.
4. **Formal warning 3:** repeated violations may lead to DLMS account deactivation and administrator review.

Serious misconduct, such as threats, targeted harassment, or exposing private information, may be referred to staff immediately. Staff may remove harmful content while reviewing a report. Warnings do not automatically remove badges or points.

## 5. Review and appeals

If you think a content block or moderation action was mistaken, contact the library team at **dlms@kvsulur.in**. Include your name, the post or reply, and a short explanation. Staff can review the context and correct an action when appropriate.

Administrators and authorized staff handle reports and moderation decisions. They should consider context, avoid penalizing normal conversation, and apply these rules consistently.
