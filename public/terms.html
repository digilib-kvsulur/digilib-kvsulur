Terms of Service

PM SHRI Kendriya Vidyalaya AFS Sulur • Digital Library Management System
Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the Digital Library Management System (dlms.kvsulur.in), you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service and all applicable library rules of PM SHRI Kendriya Vidyalaya AFS Sulur.

2. Eligible Users

The DLMS portal is intended exclusively for registered students, faculty, and administrative staff of PM SHRI Kendriya Vidyalaya AFS Sulur. Students access the service using their official school admission number or linked Google account.

3. User Responsibilities

  • Keep your library login credentials confidential. Do not share your password or linked Google account.
  • Care for all physical and digital library materials borrowed under your account.
  • Return or renew borrowed books on or before the due date to ensure fair access for all classmates.
  • Maintain respectful and academic language when submitting book reviews, discussions, or community reading posts.

4. Termination of Access

The School reserves the right to suspend or restrict access to the library portal in cases of repeated violation of library rules, misuse of the service, or withdrawal from the school.
